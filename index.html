Her Story

A life rich in family

“[A favorite saying, verse or line that captures Evelyn]”

Evelyn M. Cornett, formerly of the North Side, passed away on Sunday, September 10, 2017, at the age of 82. She was the beloved wife of the late Richard Anderson, Sr. and Ralph Cornett.

She was the loving mother of Steve Anderson (Roxann), Richard Anderson, Jr. (Maria), Maureen Warrick (the late Gregory, Sr.) and Nancy Murphy (Thomas). Her legacy lives on through her 12 grandchildren and 16 great-grandchildren.

Evelyn was the sister of Barbara Tierno and was preceded in death by 15 brothers and sisters. She is also survived by many nieces and nephews.
